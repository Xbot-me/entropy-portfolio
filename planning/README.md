# Entropy Portfolio — Planning Pack

This branch contains the pre-build plan for a portfolio driven by **Order ↔ Chaos**, with English as the main information channel, selective Bangla, and a purely procedural alien layer.

## Non-negotiable constraint

The emotional influence from *Clair Obscur: Expedition 33* is limited to **selective light and shadow, melancholy, contrast, and beauty under strain**. Do not reproduce its characters, setting, architecture, iconography, typography, menus, HUD, game-specific symbols, or signature objects. In particular, do not make the design revolve around a monolith, painter figure, numbered expedition, or Belle Époque/Art Deco imitation.

The site needs an original visual metaphor. Current proposal: **Residual Signal** — a composed editorial surface whose type, marks, and light briefly lose registration under interaction, then recover. Think damaged print, offset layers, interference maps, ink bleed, and signal traces—not a fantasy game world.

## Evidence boundary

The public site could not be fetched as rendered HTML in the current browsing session. This inventory therefore combines the supplied handoff document with the linked project's public repository READMEs and facts already supplied by the owner. It is intentionally conservative. Any detail marked **confirm** should not be promoted to a public claim until verified.

## Product goal

A senior engineer's portfolio should feel authored, not generated. It should attract attention in the first few seconds, then reward scrutiny with clear technical evidence. The experimental layer must never make the visitor fight the page to understand the work or contact the owner.
